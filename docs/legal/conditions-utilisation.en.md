## 1. What this service is

M.I.A Markets is an information and education tool about financial markets. The
service reads market data, produces a structured description of it — zones,
structure, volatility regime, macroeconomic context — and gives you a
conversational agent that explains that reading.

The service describes what it observes. It recommends nothing.

M.I.A Markets provides **no trading signal**, **no recommendation**, **no
investment advice**, and never indicates an action to take on a market. We hold
no funds, execute no orders, and never act on your behalf. The service takes no
account of your financial situation, your objectives or your experience.

**Every investment decision is entirely yours**, along with its consequences.

## 2. Risk

Trading carries a risk of loss. That risk increases with leverage, which can cost
you more than the amount you committed at the outset.

The measurements the service displays concern past situations. They do not apply
to situations still to come: a market does not repeat its past.

Commit only amounts whose loss would not change your situation.
