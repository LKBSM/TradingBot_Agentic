# M.I.A Markets — Privacy Policy

_Version 2026-09-14 · Last updated: 14 September 2026_

> English version. This is a faithful translation of the French text, which is
> the one that prevails: in case of divergence, the French version applies.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

Loukmane Bessam, operator of M.I.A Markets, is the **person in charge of the
protection of personal information**.

You can reach him at **contact@mia.markets** with any question about your
information, or to exercise the rights described in section 6.

## 2. What we collect — and nothing else

- your **email address**;
- your **account identifier** and the **session token** that keeps you signed in;
  your password is kept only in hashed form, never in clear text;
- if you choose "Continue with Google", the **Google identifier** linked to your
  account;
- your **payment customer identifier** at Stripe;
- your **subscription status** and the associated **dates** — start, renewal,
  cancellation;
- the **version and timestamp** of the documents you accepted;
- your **display preferences**, if you choose to synchronise them.

We collect **no bank card data**: it goes straight to Stripe and never passes
through our servers.

Authentication is operated by us, with no third-party processor.

## 3. Why we collect it

To **give you access to the service**, **bill you**, and **answer your support
requests**.

We do not use your information for other purposes and we sell it to no one.

## 4. Where it is hosted

Our servers are located **in the United States**. Your information is therefore
communicated and stored there, and may be subject to local law.

A privacy impact assessment is **under way** for this communication.

## 5. How long we keep it

Your account information is kept **for as long as your account exists**.

After your account is closed, it is deleted, apart from billing records, which we
must keep for the **legally required retention period for accounting documents**.

## 6. Your rights

At any time you may:

- **access** the information we hold about you;
- have it **corrected** if it is inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal;
- **withdraw your consent**;
- ask for the **deletion** of your account and the information attached to it;
- ask that your information be **communicated to you** in a structured, commonly
  used technological format.

Write to **contact@mia.markets**. We answer within **30 days**.

If our answer does not satisfy you, you may lodge a complaint with the
Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

## 7. Who else processes your information

- **Our hosting provider** — receives all account information, in order to host
  the service.
- **Stripe** — receives your email address, your customer identifier and your
  subscription status, in order to take payments.
- **Anthropic** — receives **the content of the questions you ask the agent**,
  along with the current market reading, in order to produce the answer.
- **TwelveData**, our market data provider — receives **no** personal
  information; it supplies us with prices.

About Anthropic: when you ask the agent a question, the text of your question is
sent to it in order to produce the answer. Do not write personal or sensitive
information there that you do not want leaving our hands.

## 8. Voice dictation

The service offers voice input. Speech recognition is performed by your browser;
on some browsers — Chrome or Edge, for example — the captured audio may be sent
to the browser publisher's servers to be transcribed there. Transcription is
therefore never purely local.

## 9. Confidentiality incidents

We keep a **register of confidentiality incidents**.

If an incident presenting a risk of serious injury occurs, we inform you and
notify the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec as promptly as possible.

## 10. Cookies

The service sets the cookies necessary for it to work — session, preferences.
Non-essential cookies are set only with your consent.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. The **version** and its **last updated** date appear
at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we tell you by email.

## 12. Contact us

**contact@mia.markets**
